The evil eye is one of the oldest symbols people still wear today. Across the
Mediterranean, the Middle East, and South Asia — where it's known as *nazar* — it
has been worn for generations as a token of protection. Here's what it means and
how to wear one.

## What the evil eye represents

The tradition holds that a look given out of envy or ill will can carry negative
energy — the "evil eye." The blue eye amulet is worn to reflect that gaze back,
acting as a symbolic shield. It isn't a medical or magical guarantee of anything;
it's a piece of cultural symbolism worn as a reminder that you're looking after
yourself and the people you love.

## Why it's usually blue

The classic evil eye is cobalt blue with white and black rings. Blue has long
been the colour associated with protection in the cultures that carry this
tradition, which is why the amulet has kept its look for thousands of years.

## Pairing the evil eye with stones

An evil eye works beautifully alongside natural stones that share its protective
theme. A common pairing is with **Black Tourmaline**, the grounding stone of
protection — together they read as a clear intention to stay steady and shielded.
It's also worn simply on its own, or stacked with a stone that matches whatever
else you're focused on.

## How to wear it

There's no strict rule. Many people wear the evil eye on the left wrist — the
side traditionally associated with what you receive and let in — but wear it
wherever it feels right to you. What matters is that it's visible enough to serve
its purpose as a daily reminder.

## Caring for your evil eye bracelet

If your bracelet combines an evil eye with natural stone beads, treat it like any
gemstone piece: keep it away from harsh water and chemicals, avoid prolonged
direct sunlight, and cleanse it now and then with sage smoke or moonlight. Every
OJARA evil eye piece arrives cleansed, charged, and ready to wear.

Worn honestly — as symbol and reminder rather than superstition — the evil eye is
a small, meaningful way to carry a bit of protection with you wherever you go.
