People have worn stones for their symbolism for centuries. At OJARA, each
bracelet is chosen for what it's traditionally *worn as* — a reminder of an
intention you carry through the day. None of this is a health claim; it's the
meaning and symbolism people have long attached to these stones. Here are four of
our core ones.

## Black Tourmaline — protection

Deep, opaque black, Black Tourmaline is the classic stone of protection and
grounding. It's traditionally worn as a boundary — a reminder to stay steady and
unbothered by the noise around you. Pair it with an evil eye for a doubled-down
protection piece.

## Citrine — abundance

Warm and golden, Citrine is the stone of abundance and optimism. It's worn as a
reminder to stay open to opportunity and to carry a little of its brightness into
work, money, and new beginnings. It's a natural choice if your intention this
season is growth.

## Carnelian — courage

Rich orange-red, Carnelian is the stone of courage, motivation, and vitality.
It's traditionally worn when you need a push — before something you've been
putting off, or through a stretch that asks more of you than usual. Think of it
as a wearable nudge to begin.

## Lapis Lazuli — clarity

Deep celestial blue flecked with gold, Lapis Lazuli is the stone of truth and
clarity. It's worn as a quiet anchor for a busy mind — a reminder to think
clearly, speak honestly, and cut through the clutter. A good companion for study,
decisions, and honest conversations.

## How to choose

Don't overthink it. Ask what you want to be reminded of most right now:

- **Feeling exposed or scattered?** Black Tourmaline.
- **Chasing growth or a fresh start?** Citrine.
- **Need to be braver?** Carnelian.
- **Craving a clearer head?** Lapis Lazuli.

Many people wear more than one, or stack them. There's no wrong combination — the
stone that speaks to your intention today is the right one. Every OJARA piece is
a natural stone, cleansed and charged before it reaches you, so whichever you
choose arrives ready to wear.
