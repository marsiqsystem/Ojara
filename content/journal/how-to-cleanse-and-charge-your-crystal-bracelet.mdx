Cleansing and charging are traditions people have kept with their stones for a
long time. They are not medicine and they make no promise about your body — they
are a small ritual of intention, a way of making a piece feel like *yours* before
you wear it. Here is how it's traditionally done, and how to keep your bracelet
looking its best.

## Why every OJARA piece arrives already cleansed

Before it ships, each OJARA bracelet is cleared of the energy of everyone who
handled it before you and set with intention — traditionally through sage smoke,
sound, or a night of moonlight. It reaches you ready to wear. What follows is for
keeping it that way over time.

## Ways to cleanse your bracelet

- **Sage or incense smoke.** Pass the bracelet gently through the smoke a few
  times. This is the most common method and works for every stone.
- **Moonlight.** Leave it on a windowsill overnight, ideally on a full moon. Safe
  for delicate and light-sensitive stones because there's no heat or water.
- **Sound.** A singing bowl, a bell, or any sustained tone is a gentle,
  contact-free way to reset a piece.

## Setting an intention (charging)

"Charging" simply means pairing the cleansed piece with a clear intention. Hold
the bracelet, take a slow breath, and name what you're wearing it for — focus,
calm, courage, protection. There's nothing mystical required; it's a moment of
attention that turns an accessory into a daily reminder.

## What to avoid

Crystals are more delicate than they look. A few habits protect them:

- **Skip prolonged direct sunlight.** Some stones, like amethyst and citrine, can
  fade over time in strong sun.
- **Keep them away from harsh water and chemicals.** Remove your bracelet before
  showering, swimming, or using cleaning products. Many stones are porous or
  soft, and the elastic cord dislikes repeated soaking.
- **Store it flat.** Coiled loosely in a pouch or on a dish, away from harder
  jewellery that can scratch it.

## How often?

Whenever it feels right — after an intense day, before a fresh start, or on the
new and full moon if you like to keep a rhythm. There's no rule. Cleanse when the
piece feels like it's carrying more than you'd like it to.

Treated gently, a natural gemstone bracelet will stay with you for years — a
quiet, wearable reminder of the intention you set with it.
